What does the setup fee include?

The one-time fee covers the agreed campaign strategy, setup and implementation, pre-launch checks, walkthrough and handover. The minimum campaign duration is 30 days.

How much should I budget for ads?

Regional campaigns require a minimum Meta ad budget of KSh 15,000 for 30 days. Nationwide campaigns require at least KSh 30,000 for 30 days. The advertising budget is paid separately to Meta.

Can I run ads for any product category?

Yes! We support all major categories including TV & Audio, Shoes, Phones, Appliances, Beauty, Clothes, Kids products, Automotive and more.

Can I run ads to WhatsApp instead of a website?

Yes. We can configure a Click-to-WhatsApp campaign or a website campaign as part of the agreed setup.
