FAQ

Clear answers before you start

What does the setup fee include?

Campaign strategy, setup and implementation, pre-launch checks, a walkthrough and handover. The minimum campaign is 30 days.

How much should I budget for ads?

Regional campaigns need at least KSh 15,000 in Meta ad spend for 30 days. Nationwide needs at least KSh 30,000. You pay ad spend directly to Meta.

Why only 3 setups per day?

Each setup includes pre-launch checks, a walkthrough and handover, so we cap new setups at 3 per day to give every store proper attention.

WhatsApp or website: which should I choose?

Either works. We set up Click-to-WhatsApp or website campaigns depending on how your customers order.

Can I run ads for any product category?

Yes, all major categories: electronics, shoes, clothes, phones, appliances, beauty, kids' items, automotive and more.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Results vary by product, price, stock and budget. We build a solid foundation and measure what works.
