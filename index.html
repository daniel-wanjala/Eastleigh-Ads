Common Questions

Everything you need to know about WhatsApp Flows

Do I need WhatsApp Business API? +

Yes, WhatsApp Flows only work with WhatsApp Business API accounts (not the regular Business App). We can help you set this up if you don't have it yet.

How long does it take to build a Flow? +

Most Flows are completed within 2-5 business days depending on complexity. Simple booking forms can be done in 2 days, while complex multi-step flows with integrations take 4-5 days.

Can you integrate with my existing systems? +

Absolutely! We can connect your Flows to Google Sheets, CRMs (HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce), payment gateways, custom APIs, and most popular business tools via webhooks.

Do you provide training and support? +

Yes! Every package includes training on how to manage your Flows. We also provide post-launch support (duration depends on package) and offer optional monthly maintenance plans.

Can Flows accept M-Pesa payments? +

Yes! We integrate M-Pesa STK Push so customers can pay directly within the WhatsApp conversation. Available in Professional and Enterprise packages.

What if I want to make changes later? +

We offer Flow updates and modifications at KSh 5,000-15,000 depending on the scope of changes. Or you can opt for a monthly maintenance plan that includes unlimited minor updates.
