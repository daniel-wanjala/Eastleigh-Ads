FAQs

Before you get started.

Does KSh 20,000 include my advertising budget?

No. KSh 20,000 is the one-time setup fee. You pay Meta for ad spend separately through your own ad account.

Is ongoing ad management included?

No. Ongoing management is a separate service with a separate fee. We can discuss the right level of support for your business before you commit.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. No responsible setup can guarantee sales. Results depend on factors including the product, pricing, creative, audience, budget, stock, delivery and how enquiries are converted into orders.

What do I need before we start?

Ideally, a live product catalogue or website, clear pricing, product photos or videos, delivery details, access to the relevant Meta business assets and a way to handle customer enquiries.

Can we use WhatsApp instead of a website?

Yes, where it fits your sales process. We can discuss a WhatsApp-focused destination or a website path during the initial review.
